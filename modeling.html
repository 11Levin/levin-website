Timing Tells and Decision-Making in High-Stakes Poker Tournaments

  • Slower decision times leaned toward folding rather than calling, although timing alone was not statistically definitive among elite professionals.
  • Earlier aggressive actions strongly predicted continued aggression on later streets, while chip standing did not dictate aggression.
  • Objective hand strength and final winners diverged most pre-flop and on the flop, making early streets the strongest window for successful bluffs.

STOR 538 Playoff Project: NBA Predictions

  • A simple linear model using four core differential variables outperformed point-spread baselines, with a threshold adjustment helping most in close games.
  • For total points, nonlinear models outperformed linear regression, with a stacking ensemble of Gradient Boosted Trees and Random Forest capturing the scoring environment best.
  • For offensive rebounds, a basic three-variable linear model outperformed more complex neural-network and time-series approaches.

Modeling to Predict Scooby-Doo Ratings

  • Elements that lowered ratings before 1995, including Batman guest appearances and real monsters, became positive rating drivers after 1995.
  • Daphne-focused actions consistently improved episode ratings across eras, while prominent Velma actions consistently lowered them.
